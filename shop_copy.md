# Shop copy — paste these into Etsy by hand

Everything here is UI-only. Etsy's v3 API cannot write the About page, the
policies, the FAQ, the icon or the banner, so this is the manual 10 minutes.

Already set by API, nothing to do: **shop title, announcement, message to
buyers, the three sections, and all 24 listings assigned to them.**

---

## 1. Icon and banner

**Shop Manager → your shop name (top left) → Edit shop**

- **Shop icon** → upload `designs/brand/icon.png` (500x500)
- **Banner** → choose the big banner option → upload `designs/brand/banner.png` (1600x400)

---

## 2. About section

**Shop Manager → Edit shop → About your shop**

Two fields here, not one.

### Headline (max 150)

```
Printable plant care cards that tell you what to check, not what day to water
```

### Your story (max 5000)

Apostrophes and quotes are fine in these fields. The `&#39;` escaping problem
only affects text written through the API, not text typed into Etsy's own forms.

```
I kept buying houseplants and then forgetting which one wanted to dry out between waterings and which one wanted to stay damp. The little tag that comes stuck in the pot says something like "water weekly" — which is wrong in most homes and useless in all of them.

So these cards say what to check instead of what day to do it. Pot size, light and season all change how fast a plant drinks, so a fixed schedule is the most reliable way to drown something. Each card tells you what "dry enough" actually looks like for that specific plant, what the early warning signs of trouble are, and what to do about them.

Every card also says plainly whether the plant is toxic to cats and dogs. That turns out to be weirdly hard to find at the moment you need it, which is usually while you're standing in a shop holding the plant.

Each one is a 4x6 PDF plus a matching PNG. Print it, prop it against the pot, stop guessing.
```

## 3. Shop policies

**Shop Manager → Settings → Policies**

### Returns & exchanges

Select: **I don't accept returns, exchanges, or cancellations**

Then in "Other" / additional notes:

> Because these are instant digital downloads, they cannot be returned once
> the files have been accessed.
>
> That said, if a file will not open, will not print correctly, or is not what
> the listing described, message me. I will fix it or refund you. I would much
> rather sort out a problem than have you stuck with something that does not
> work.

### Privacy

> I only ever see what Etsy shows me about an order, which is your name and
> the details needed to deliver your download. I do not collect anything else,
> I do not use tracking of my own, and I do not share, sell or pass your
> information to anyone.
>
> Etsy handles payment and stores your order. Their privacy policy covers that
> part.

## 4. FAQ

**Shop Manager → Edit shop → FAQs** (one question and answer per entry)

**Q: What exactly do I get?**
> A print-ready PDF at 4x6 inches and 300 DPI, plus the same design as a
> high-resolution PNG. Both download instantly after purchase. Nothing is
> posted to you.

**Q: How do I print it?**
> Choose 4x6 and print at 100 percent scale. Turn off any fit-to-page or
> shrink-to-fit option, as those resize the layout. Photo paper and cardstock
> both work well, and any drugstore photo kiosk prints this size.

**Q: Can I print it bigger?**
> Yes. The files are 300 DPI, so they stay sharp at 5x7. Past that the text
> starts to look soft, as the design is laid out for a small card.

**Q: Is this plant safe for my cat or dog?**
> Every card states it plainly, and the pet-safe plants have their own section
> in the shop. Note that "toxic" usually means mouth irritation and an upset
> stomach rather than anything worse, but if your animal has actually eaten
> part of a plant, call your vet rather than relying on a printable.

**Q: Why does the card not tell me to water every 7 days?**
> Because that would be wrong for most homes. How fast a plant dries out
> depends on pot size, light, airflow and season. A fixed schedule is the most
> common way people drown a houseplant, so the cards describe what to check
> instead.

**Q: Can I use these in my plant shop or give them to customers?**
> Personal use and gifts are fine. For anything commercial, including bundling
> cards with plants you sell, message me first.

**Q: Can you add a plant that is not in the shop?**
> Ask. I am adding species regularly and I will prioritise ones people
> actually request.
